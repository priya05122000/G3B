Study Abroad in Netherlands with G3B Education

The Netherlands presents an alluring mix of elements that draw international students. It offers excellent quality of education, a thriving global community and numerous chances for professional advancements. The nation is also a particularly desirable choice because of its dedication to innovation, it’s rather low cost of living in comparison to other European states and its English taught curricula.

Sought after courses in Netherlands

The Netherland offers HBO Programs (Hoger Beroepsonderwijs) which means higher professional education having a duration of 04 years which emphasis on applied skills and – preparation of specific professions.

    • Engineering – Mechanical, Electrical & Electronics, Construction Management, Automotive, Etc.
    • Technology – Computer Science, Data Science, Information Technology, Artificial Intelligence
    • Management – Project Management, Operations Managements, Marketing, Accounting & Finances, Analytics, Business management etc. (*GMAT is required by certain B Schools)
    • Agriculture – Agriculture Science.
    • Hospitality – International Hospitality, Tourism, Catering etc.
    • Natural Sciences – Earth Science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics
    • Health Management – Health Care, Elder Care, Dentistry, Mental Health
    • Arts – Performing Arts, Fine Arts

Why should you study in the Netherlands?

  • Affordable Costs: In comparison to other study destinations, the cost of tuition and living expenditures in the Netherlands is fairly reasonable. This country offers monetary aid programs and several lodging alternatives with relatively low costs.
  • Short Duration Programs: The Dutch universities offer many short term programs characteristically at the Masters level which range from 1–2 years, thus helping students to look for employment at the earliest.
  • Global and Multicultural Atmosphere: The Netherlands exhibits a warm and welcoming attitude towards international students and hosts diverse cultural backgrounds which blend seamlessly with the local culture. English being widely spoken in the Netherlands it becomes stress-free to connect with the people from all over the world.
  • High-Quality Education: The Netherlands offers top notch education as it hosts many top-ranked universities and research establishments. The Dutch education system is known for its pioneering education methodologies and highlights imagination and problem resolving aids, organizing students for an international career.
  • Safe and Happy Country: The Netherlands one among the top five happy countries in the world. The local law and order is excellent making it one of the safest country to stay in.

Scholarships Offered in the Netherlands

NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship): For non-EEA students, and offers tuition and living cost support.
Orange Tulip Scholarship: Exclusive for Indian students offers full or partial funding at Dutch universities, as per SIEC India.
Leiden University Excellence Scholarships (LexS): Offers tuition fee scholarships for Master’s students.
Utrecht University Excellence Scholarships: Scholarships for international Master’s students.
Radboud University Scholarship Programme: Offers scholarships to non-EEA Master’s students.
TU Delft Excellence Scholarships: Scholarships for top international Master’s students in engineering.
Maastricht University High Potential Scholarships: Offers scholarships for international students.
VU Amsterdam Fellowship Programme (VUFP): Offers tuition fee waivers and can be combined with the NL Scholarship.
Erasmus University MBA Scholarship: Offers MBA scholarships.
Eric Bleumink Scholarships: Offered by the University of Groningen.
National Overseas Scholarship Scheme: This scheme is another option for Indian students looking to study abroad.
Private Scholarships: Options like the JN Tata Endowment Loan Scholarship.
