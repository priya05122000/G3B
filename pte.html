PTE - Pearson Test of English

Looking to study or work abroad? The Pearson Test of English (PTE) is a globally accepted English language proficiency exam that evaluates your ability to read, write, listen, and speak in English. Recognized by thousands of universities, governments, and institutions, the PTE is a smart choice for students and professionals.

About the PTE Exam

  • Testing Authority : Pearson, Australia
  • Official Website : [https://www.pearsonpte.com/](https://www.pearsonpte.com/)
  • Exam Mode : Computer-Based
  • Exam Duration : 2 hours 45 minutes
  • Identity Proof Required : Valid Passport
  • Eligibility : Minimum Qualification - Class 10 and above
  • Test Validity : 2 Years
  • Test Fee (Approx) : ₹17,000 (as of 2024)

PTE Exam Structure

The PTE evaluates your English proficiency through four key sections.

  • Reading
  • Writing
  • Listening
  • Speaking

Each section is integrated into real-life academic and professional English usage, making it ideal for higher education and work settings.

Where is PTE Accepted?

PTE scores are accepted by.

  • Universities and colleges in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and the EU.
  • Visa and immigration authorities in Australia and New Zealand.
  • Various employers and organizations globally.

Whether you're applying for student visas or professional roles abroad, PTE can open doors to global opportunities.

Scoring System

  • Minimum Recommended Score : 65 out of 90.
  • Scores are reported on a Global Scale of English,offering a detailed analysis of your English abilities.

Retake, Reschedule & Cancellation

  • You can retake the PTE if you're not satisfied with your score.
  • Options to reschedule or cancel your test are available — refer to the official [PTE website] (https://www.pearsonpte.com/) for the latest policies and fees.
