Scholarships Offered in USA

Fulbright-Nehru Fellowships: These esteemed fellowships provide complete funding for postgraduate studies, research, and teaching in the United States and are jointly funded by the governments of the United States and India. They pay for living expenses, education, and travel.
Tata Scholarship for Cornell University: This scholarship supports Indian students pursuing undergraduate studies at Cornell University.
Inlaks Shivdasani Scholarships: These scholarships are offered to Indian students for pursuing graduate and postgraduate studies in the US or Europe.
Rotary International Ambassadorial Scholarships: Rotary International offers these scholarships to outstanding young leaders from developing countries, including India, to pursue Master's degrees in the US.
AAUW International Fellowships: These fellowships support outstanding women from around the world, including India, who wish to pursue postgraduate studies in the United States.
