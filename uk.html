Why Should You Study in the UK?

  • Excellent Education: The second best higher education system in the world is found in the United Kingdom.The Research Excellence Framework lists with almost over 80-82% of the research conducted by UK institutions as "globally exceptional."
  • Reputed Institutions: The UK hosts four of the top ten universities worldwide, as per the QS World University Rankings 2025.
  • Multifarious Course Offerings: UK universities provide a wide range of courses with chances for early specialization. Students can choose from over 1,000 plus courses covering more than 20 subject topics.
  • Short Duration of Degree: UK degrees are budget friendly and can save a lot of money as they are shorter than those in many other countries. While most undergraduate programs take three years to complete, postgraduate programs usually takes only one year.
  • Part-Time Job Opportunities: International students are allowed to work up to 18-20 hours per week during the school year and full-time during the holidays.

Scholarships Offered in the UK

Commonwealth Scholarship and Fellowship Plan: Master's and doctoral courses (for study in the UK and distance learning), plus academic, professional and medical fellowships.
Chevening Scholarship: For professionals, the selection is done by the high commission and British embassies.
GREAT Scholarships: Scholarships for one-year master's courses across a range of subjects at a variety of UK universities.
British Council Scholarships for Women in STEM: Scholarships for women undertaking master's degrees in a science, technology, engineering or mathematics course at one of 19 UK universities.
Global Wales Scholarships: Global Wales offers a variety of scholarship opportunities for international students.
