GRE – Graduate Record Examination (General Test)

Looking to pursue a postgraduate degree at a top global university? The GRE (Graduate Record Examination) is your gateway to master’s and doctoral programs in countries like the USA, UK, Canada, and more. Accepted by thousands of institutions worldwide, the GRE evaluates your readiness for advanced academic study.

About the GRE Exam

  • Testing Authority : ETS (Educational Testing Service), USA
  • Official Website : https://www.ets.org/
  • Exam Mode : Computer-Based Test (CBT)
  • Exam Duration : 2 Hours
  • Identity Proof Required : Valid Passport
  • Eligibility : Graduation (Bachelor’s degree or equivalent)
  • Test Validity : 5 Years
  • Approximate Test Fee : ₹22,550 (as of 2024)

GRE Exam Format

The GRE General Test measures your academic skills through three key sections:

  • Verbal Reasoning: Assesses your ability to understand written material and analyze relationships among words and concepts.
  • Quantitative Reasoning (Maths): Evaluates basic math skills and your understanding of elementary mathematical concepts.
  • Analytical Writing (Essay): Tests your ability to articulate complex ideas clearly and effectively.

Where is GRE Accepted?

The GRE is widely accepted for:

  • Postgraduate (PG) program admissions in USA, Canada, UK, Australia, and Europe.
  • Some universities and business schools in India.
  • Scholarships, assistantships, and fellowships abroad

Whether you’re applying to an MBA, MS, or PhD program, the GRE can significantly strengthen your application profile.

Scoring System

  • Total Score Range: 260 to 340.
  • The Verbal and Quantitative sections are each scored between 130–170
  • The Analytical Writing section is scored separately on a scale of 0–6

A good GRE score boosts your chances of admission and scholarships at leading global institutions.

Retake, Reschedule & Cancellation

  • You can retake the GRE once every 21 days (up to 5 times in a rolling 12-month period).
  • Rescheduling or canceling the exam is possible—check the latest rules and fees on the official GRE website.
