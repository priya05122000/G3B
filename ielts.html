IELTS – International English Language Testing System

Planning to study or work abroad? The IELTS (International English Language Testing System) is one of the most trusted English proficiency exams accepted by universities, employers, and immigration authorities across the globe. Whether you're aiming for higher education or skilled migration, IELTS is your key to global opportunities.

About the IELTS Exam

  • Testing Authority : Cambridge ESOL Examinations, UK
  • Registration Website : https://www.ieltsidpindia.com/
  • Exam Mode : Computer-Based & Paper-Based
  • Exam Duration : 2 hours 45 minutes
  • Identity Proof Required : Valid Passport
  • Eligibility : Class 10 and above
  • Test Validity : 2 Years
  • Approximate Test Fee : ₹17,000 (as of 2024)

IELTS Exam Format

IELTS assesses your English skills in real-world academic and professional contexts through four sections:

  • Reading: Evaluates your ability to understand passages and answer comprehension questions.
  • Writing: Tests your grammar, structure, and ability to express ideas clearly in two writing tasks.
  • Listening: Measures how well you understand spoken English in conversations and monologues.
  • Speaking: A face-to-face interview to assess your verbal fluency, pronunciation, and coherence.

You can choose between Academic IELTS (for study) and General Training IELTS (for work/migration).

Where is IELTS Accepted?

IELTS scores are accepted by.

  • Educational institutions in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and the EU.
  • Employers and government agencies for immigration and work visas.
  • Over 11,000 institutions worldwide.

Scoring System

  • Score Range: 1 to 9 Bands.
  • Minimum Recommended Score: 6.5 Band for academic purposes
  • Each section is scored individually, and an overall band score is calculated

Higher scores increase your chances of acceptance at top universities and better job opportunities abroad.

Retake, Reschedule & Cancellation

  • You can retake the IELTS anytime—there's no mandatory waiting period.
  • Rescheduling or canceling your test is possible—refer to the official IELTS IDP website for current policies and fees
