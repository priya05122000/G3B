TOEFL – Test of English as a Foreign Language

Planning to study or work abroad in an English-speaking country? The TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language) is one of the most widely recognized English language proficiency tests in the world. Accepted by universities, immigration authorities, and employers globally, TOEFL helps open doors to your international future.

About the TOEFL Exam

  • Testing Authority: ETS (Educational Testing Service), USA
  • Registration Website: ets.org
  • Exam Mode: Computer-Based
  • Exam Duration: 2 Hours 15 Minutes
  • Identity Proof Required: Valid Passport
  • Eligibility: Minimum Qualification – Class 10 and above
  • Test Validity: 2 Years
  • Approximate Test Fee: ₹17,000 (as of 2024)

TOEFL Exam Format

TOEFL measures your ability to use and understand English at the university level across the following sections:

  • Reading: Tests your ability to understand and analyze academic texts.
  • Writing: Assesses your ability to present ideas clearly in written English.
  • Listening: Evaluates comprehension of lectures, conversations, and discussions.

Where is TOEFL Accepted?

TOEFL scores are widely accepted by:

  • Universities and colleges in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and the EU.
  • Institutions offering undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional programs.
  • Government agencies and employers requiring English proficiency for immigration or work.

Scoring System

  • Total Score: Out of 120.
  • Minimum Recommended Score: 75
  • Each section is scored individually and contributes to the overall score
  • Detailed feedback helps identify strengths and areas for improvement

Retake, Reschedule & Cancellation

  • You can retake the TOEFL after a 3-day waiting period.
  • You may reschedule or cancel the test as per ETS policies—visit the official TOEFL website for details
